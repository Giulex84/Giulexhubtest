Gameplay

Classic mode provides casual progression. Daily Arena is a skill-based challenge whose deck, moves and results are validated by the backend. Technical limits, availability controls and anti-abuse measures may apply. Server-verified Daily Arena completions may appear in a daily Top 10 showing the authorized Pi username, moves and score. Async PvP uses the same server-controlled deck for both players and may match a user with another Pioneer or a clearly identified Arena Bot. PvP has no entry fee or prize in Pi. Users may not automate play, manipulate requests or exploit defects.

Premium

Premium costs exactly 1 Test-Pi and is activated only after the backend verifies successful completion through Pi Network. It is restored using the verified app-specific Pi UID.

Daily Replay Tickets

A Daily Replay Ticket costs exactly 0.1 Test-Pi and permits one additional Daily Arena challenge. Each ticket is consumed when the new challenge is generated. It does not provide Pi or a prize.

No prizes or wagering

Daily Arena awards gameplay points only. It does not award Pi, money or anything of monetary value. Arena Test is not an investment, gambling or earning product.

Streaks and sharing

Completion streaks, rankings, points and shared results are entertainment features only and have no monetary value. Sharing is optional and occurs only when the user selects the share control.
