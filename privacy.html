Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Giulex Hub Test ("we", "our", or "the App") is a testing environment designed for the Pi Network Testnet. This policy explains how we handle user data.

1. Data Collection

We only collect public information provided by the Pi Network SDK, such as your Username and UID. This data is used exclusively to facilitate and verify Testnet transactions.

2. Data Usage

Your data is used solely to demonstrate and test payment flows (U2A and A2U) within the Pi Browser. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

This app uses the Pi Network SDK. By using this app, you also agree to the SocialChain Privacy Policy.
