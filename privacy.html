Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Arena Test is a Pi Testnet application for testing Pi authentication, skill-based gameplay and Test-Pi purchases.

Pi authentication

After authorization, Arena Test sends the Pi access token to its backend for verification with Pi Network’s /v2/me endpoint. It uses the app-specific Pi UID and authorized username. The browser is not treated as the authoritative source of identity.

Gameplay records

The service stores Classic level, lives and score, plus the current Daily Arena deck, revealed or matched cards, move count, status, result and completion streak. These records are associated with the verified app-specific Pi UID. Arena Test also stores the authorized Pi username, daily attempt count and best verified result, and displays the username, moves and score in the daily Top 10 leaderboard. Daily Arena data is retained temporarily to resume and validate the daily challenge. Completion streaks may be retained longer to preserve progress. Async PvP records include match identifiers, shared deck state, matched cards, moves, score, status, opponent type and authorized usernames, retained temporarily to complete asynchronous matches.

Usage metrics and Classic ranking

The Classic weekly leaderboard displays the authorized username, level and score. New Testnet analytics collection is disabled. Historical telemetry retains its existing retention; access tokens and wallet credentials are never included.

Optional sharing

After completing a Daily challenge, you may choose to share a result and Arena link through your device. Arena never sends a share automatically, and the prepared text does not include your Pi username.

Testnet payments

For Premium and Daily Replay Ticket purchases, Arena Test processes Pi payment and blockchain transaction identifiers required to approve, complete, verify and recover the purchase. Test-Pi has no real-world value. Daily Arena does not award Pi. A Daily Replay Ticket is a consumable entitlement that permits one additional challenge and is stored until used.

Security and providers

Arena Test uses Pi Network for authentication and Testnet payments and a server-side Redis-compatible store for gameplay, entitlement and payment-protection records. Server credentials remain server-side. Arena Test never asks users for a wallet passphrase, private key or seed phrase.

Weekly cosmetic awards

From the UTC week of October 12, 2026, Classic weekly top-three finishes award permanent cosmetic badges, including tied ranks. Your authorized username, weekly score and placement may appear in the Hall of Fame. Weekly award records and badge-eligible results are retained to preserve this history; these awards have no cash or Pi value and do not certify competitive fairness. Contact us using the address above for profile or history correction/deletion requests.

Analytics collection update

New Testnet analytics collection is disabled. Previously collected analytics remain subject to their existing retention. Gameplay, payment test receipts and security controls continue to operate separately.

Attendance tickets and weekly Daily simulation

Verified sign-in stores the last UTC login day, consecutive-day count and total earned Replay Tickets against the app-specific Pi UID. A free ticket is credited once every seven consecutive login days. This functional reward record is separate from disabled analytics and is retained to preserve earned credits and prevent duplicate grants.

The Testnet weekly Daily simulation records only original free completed attempts: completion day, moves, score and completion time. Per-attempt and aggregate ranking data expire after 90 days. Frozen simulated weekly winners and Classic badges may be retained longer to preserve history and prevent duplicate awards. Public rankings show authorized usernames and results, not private account identifiers. No real Pi or Test-Pi payouts are made. Contact the address above for correction or deletion requests.
