Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Giulex Hub Arena ("the App") is a gaming environment designed for the Pi Network Testnet. This policy explains how we handle user data.

1. Data Collection

We collect public information provided by the Pi Network SDK, including your Username and UID. We also temporarily track your in-game level and combo streaks to verify reward eligibility.

2. Data Usage

Your data is used solely to facilitate gameplay, verify Premium status for 10 levels, and process reward payments (A2U). We do not sell, share, or store your personal data longer than necessary for the session.

3. Third-Party Services

This app uses the Pi Network SDK. By using this app, you also agree to the SocialChain Privacy Policy.
