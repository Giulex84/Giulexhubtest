How to play

Classic: match pairs. A wrong pair costs one life; a match restores one life, up to ten. Consecutive matches build a combo. Restarting a level is free.

Daily: one free challenge per UTC day, six pairs and eighteen moves. Two cards count as one move. Resume your saved challenge during the same UTC day.

PvP: an asynchronous duel. Both players use the same deck and can play at different times. Complete the board; fewer moves wins, then score breaks a tie. A completed board beats an unfinished board.

Progress and purchases belong to your verified Pi account. Classic now saves the current board too. Avoid playing Classic on two devices at once.
